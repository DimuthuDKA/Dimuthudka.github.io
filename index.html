About Me

Welcome to my academic website. I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Hampton University, USA. Prior to joining Hampton University, I served as a Postdoctoral Researcher under Dr. Myunghee Kim in the Rehabilitation Robotics Laboratory at the University of Illinois Chicago. I earned my PhD in Computer Science at DePaul University, USA, under the supervision of Dr. Iyad Kanj and Dr. Isuru Godage. I also hold an MS in Electronics and Automation from the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka; an MS in Renewable Energy from the University of Agder, Norway; and a BS in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Ruhuna, Sri Lanka. My research interests span robotics, mechatronics, and artificial intelligence, with a focus on multidisciplinary applications.
